### 0.0.1 The Moment That Started Everything

Let's share an impossible moment together.

Look at water. Any water. A pond. A puddle. A glass.

The reflection is a natural phenomenon we take for granted.

But stop and actually see it:

- The surface generates a **dynamic** image
- In **constant** flux
- The **inverse opposite** of what it faces
- Copying movements in **real time**
- Like a movie

This is impossible.

We all know how movies are made. Cameras. Storage. Playback.

But the water has no camera. No storage. No playback mechanism.

**Yet there it is.**

---

### 0.0.2 Why This Question Matters

In one way, we do not need proof. We can see the reflection.

So why look for explanation?

For the same reason Isaac Newton did.

It is impossible for an apple to fall without explanation. Newton was working on gravity, not apples falling out of trees.

In the same way, this work is on **dynamism**, not reflections.

The reflection is the phenomenon that revealed the question.
The question is: **How does identity persist through dynamic resolution?**

### 0.0.5 What Classical Optics Does Not Explain

Classical optics tells us:

- Angles of reflection (Snell's Law)
- Intensity distributions (Fresnel equations)
- Interference patterns (wave optics)

Classical optics does **not** tell us:

- Why the reflected tree is recognizably the same tree
- Why identity persists under continuous deformation
- Why there is no storage yet perfect synchronization
- Why ripples distort but do not destroy

These are treated as given.

**They are not given. They require explanation.**

The synchronization is not mysterious.

Of course they match.
They are the same field.
